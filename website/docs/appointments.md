# Appointments

Appointments are one-off meetings with RSVP. Use them to schedule calls or syncs with specific teammates.

## Creating an appointment

1. Go to **Appointments** → "New appointment".
2. Fill in the title, optional description, date, start time, and end time.
3. Select attendees from the list of users.
4. Click **Create** — attendees receive an email invite automatically.

## Accepting or declining

Attendees see pending appointments on their **Appointments** page. Click **Accept** or **Decline** to RSVP.

## Viewing times in your timezone

All appointment times are shown in your local timezone (from your profile). The organizer's timezone is shown in parentheses for reference.

## Reminders

You'll receive a reminder email the morning before any accepted appointment (daily at 08:00 UTC).

## Deleting an appointment

Only the organizer can delete an appointment. This removes all attendee records too.

# Invitations

Invite specific people to your organization by email.

## Sending an invitation

1. Go to **Organization** → "Invite a member".
2. Enter the person's email address.
3. Optionally select a team to add them to on join.
4. Click **Create invite link**.

An email is sent to the invitee with a link to accept. The link is also copied to your clipboard as a backup.

## Accepting an invitation

1. Click the link in the invitation email.
2. Sign in (or create an account) with the **same email** the invitation was sent to.
3. Click **Accept** — you'll be added to the organization and redirected to your dashboard.

## Pending invitations

The **Pending invitations** section on the Organization page lists all invitations that haven't been accepted yet. You can copy the accept link again from here if needed.

## Invitation expiry

Invitations expire after 48 hours. If an invitation expires, the admin needs to send a new one.

## Alternative: Join codes

If you're onboarding a large group, use [join codes](./join-codes) instead — one code for the whole cohort.

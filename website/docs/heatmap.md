# Heatmap

The heatmap shows when your team members are available — all converted to your local timezone.

## Reading the heatmap

- Each cell represents a 30-minute window across the week (Monday–Sunday).
- The darker the cell, the more people are free in that window.
- **Golden hours** (highlighted cells) are when 50%+ of the selected group are available — ideal meeting times.

## Scoping the heatmap

Use the scope picker at the top to view availability for:
- **All users** — everyone in the system
- **Your organization** — just your org's members
- **A specific team** — e.g. just Engineering
- **A specific product** — the cross-cutting product group

## Setting your availability

Go to **My Slots** to add your recurring weekly availability. Times are entered in your own timezone — the heatmap handles conversion automatically.

## Timezones

If you change your timezone in **My Slots**, your existing slots are reinterpreted in the new timezone on the next heatmap refresh.

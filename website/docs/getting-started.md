# Getting started

Slot Scheduler helps distributed intern teams share weekly availability and find the best times to meet — across any timezones.

## Sign up

1. Go to the app and click **Sign up**.
2. Enter your name, email, and password.
3. Your timezone is detected automatically from your browser. You can change it later in **My Slots**.

You can also sign in with Google or GitHub if those options appear.

## What you can do

| Feature | Where |
|---------|-------|
| Set your weekly availability | My Slots |
| See when your team overlaps | Heatmap |
| Schedule one-off meetings | Appointments |
| Manage your organization | Organization |

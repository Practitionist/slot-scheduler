# Create an organization

An organization is the top-level container for your team. It holds members, teams, products, and invitations.

## Creating your first organization

1. Go to **Organization** in the sidebar.
2. Enter an organization name.
3. Pick your starter teams (Engineering, UI/UX, Testing, etc.) or add custom ones.
4. Click **Create organization**.

## Teams vs Products

- **Teams** are hierarchical groups within your org (e.g. Engineering, Design).
- **Products** are cross-cutting groups that can span multiple teams (e.g. a product that has engineers, designers, and testers).

You can use both, or just one, depending on how your organization is structured.

## Adding more teams

After your org is created, go to **Organization** → "New team" to add more.

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
|------|--------|
| **owner** | Everything, including deleting the org and transferring ownership |
| **admin** | Invite members, manage teams/products, create join codes |
| **member** | View the heatmap, create appointments, set availability |
